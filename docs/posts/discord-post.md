**About the Project**

**Graduation Scout** decides who gets a token's graduation airdrop on vibe/vibe: holders, not farms and flippers. Wallets opt in once and get an explainable score; creators pick the rules; at graduation the list is sealed with a Merkle root and paid by our ScoutDrop contract.

Every score shows its reasons. No black box.

**What's Working Now**

- Live indexing of real vibe/vibe launches, trades and graduations
- $SCOUT graduated and Scout paid its drop: 8.93M SCOUT to 183 wallets, every claim verified on chain (https://explorer.testnet.chain.robinhood.com/tx/0xf13bd23a93964ec9ab7aded3773a573adfbe3c89ef6ef71ed82d45554018699c)
