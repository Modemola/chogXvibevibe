# Graduation Scout: 90-second demo script

Open the app at https://vibevibe-gules.vercel.app/?tour=1 (or click **Watch the 2-min tour** on the home page). The guided tour drives the pages
for you; the script below is what to say over it. Arrow keys skip steps, space pauses, Esc ends the tour.
Scrolling or touching also pauses it, so keep hands off the trackpad while recording.

The tour runs about 65 seconds on its own. The script is timed to 90 seconds, so press space to pause where marked.

| Time | Screen (tour step) | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Home, mercury orb (01 *Scout, in two minutes*) | "When a token graduates on vibe/vibe, the creator airdrops to the community. Today most of that drop goes to farms and dumpers. Graduation Scout fixes it." |
| 0:10 to 0:25 | The Sieve (02 *How it works*) | "Wallets opt in by signing. Scout scores them, and farms cluster in rose and fall away. The survivors become a Merkle tree, and after the drop we watch who held." |
| 0:25 to 0:40 | Policy builder, Hold or Die (03 *Creators design the drop*) | "The creator chooses who gets the drop: a template or their own filters. The preview resolves the real allocation live. Lock it, and the policy hash goes on-chain with the drop." |
| 0:40 to 0:52 | Holder profile (04 *Every score explains itself*) | "Every score explains itself. These reason codes add up exactly to the number. This wallet held every drop it got." |
| 0:52 to 1:02 | Dumper profile (05 *Dumping follows you*) | "This one sold inside the hour and sits in a funding cluster. Negative score, so next time it gets nothing. Dumping follows you." |
| 1:02 to 1:15 | Launch aftermath (06 *Every drop gets a public report card*) | "Every drop gets a public report card: retention at one hour, a day, a week and a month, wallet by wallet. That history feeds the next score, so every drop makes the next one smarter." |
| 1:15 to 1:30 | Final call to action (07) | "In our simulated world, farms get zero percent with zero false positives, and holders get the lion's share. The ScoutDrop contract is ready. Graduation Scout: curate a community, not a dump." |

## Recording tips

- Record at 1920×1080 in a fresh browser window, at 100% zoom, with the cursor parked off-screen.
- Start the app with `SCOUT_SIMULATE=1` so the simulated world and its aftermath are loaded.
- Pause (space) on steps 04 and 06 if you need more time to finish a line; press → to continue.
- The evaluation numbers come from the simulated world (README, *Evaluation on the simulated world*); say "simulated" when you quote them.
