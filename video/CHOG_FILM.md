# "Hold Your Chog": creative brief

**Format:** 2:00 · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · H.264 · original synthesized soundtrack · under 30 MB to share in chat and on X.
**Audience:** Chog holders, Chogathon judges, vibe/vibe creators. Many are watching on a phone with sound on.
**One line:** *Hold your Chog, and the airdrop finds you.*

## The prompt

> Make a 2-minute motion-graphics film that feels like a Saturday-morning cartoon made by a crypto-native design studio.
> The stars are the real Chog Genesis NFTs: their actual artwork, pulled from the collection's on-chain metadata,
> mounted as glossy die-cut stickers and as 3D collectible cards that spin, bounce and catch the light. Nothing about
> them is redrawn or invented.
>
> Tell one story: a token graduates on vibe/vibe, the airdrop rains down, and flippers and farm clones grab the coins
> and run. Then Graduation Scout rolls in: a small silver orb, curious like a robot pet. It reads each Chog straight
> from Monad (same wallet, no bridge), stamps a passport, and watches who holds. The Chogs who stay get the drop, and
> the long-time Chogs get extra. Flippers, ruggers and farms bounce off a shield and get nothing.
>
> The tone is cute, bouncy and confident, never cheesy. Every movement has squash and stretch and lands on the beat.
> Soft pastel light on a deep Monad-purple night: lavender, bubblegum pink, butter yellow, mint. Rounded type
> (Fredoka) that pops in with a wobble. Sparkles, confetti and 3D gold coins, used where they reward the viewer,
> not everywhere.
>
> Teach exactly three facts and make them unforgettable:
> 1. Your Chog is your passport: same address on Monad and vibe/vibe, nothing to bridge.
> 2. Holding raises your score: +0.10 for a Chog, +0.05 more after 30 days.
> 3. The Chog Holders recipe: 60% to Chogs who don't dump, 25% extra for long-time Chogs, 15% to proven holders.
>    Dumpers, ruggers and farms: 0.
>
> Every number on screen is real or labelled as an illustration. Never imply Chog or vibe/vibe endorse the product.
> End on the address, a wall of Chogs, and one line that makes people smile.

## Rules

- **The Chogs:** the artwork comes from the collection's own metadata (`tokenURI` on Monad, `0xc96d…3763`), shown
  as-is, with each Chog's real number.
- **Numbers:** facts from the product (the recipe, the boosts, the $SCOUT drop) or clearly labelled simulations.
- **Fonts:** Fredoka for the fun type, JetBrains Mono for numbers and labels.
- **Pacing:** something new reaches the eye every bar, and there are no more than about eight words on screen at once.
